# Privacy

Batch Resize Video is developed by [3thousand30](https://3thousand30.com). Resizing happens locally through the FFmpeg engine bundled with the app.

3thousand30 does not collect, store, or receive your videos, filenames, resize settings, or outputs. The app stores ordinary local interface preferences only, such as the selected theme.

FFmpeg licence information is included with the installed app.
